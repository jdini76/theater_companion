# Tutorial 01 · Introduction to RehearseLines

Narrator: Emily (ElevenLabs)

## 1. Introduction to RehearseLines

Welcome to RehearseLines. If you've ever tried to practice a scene on your own, you know how easy it is to lose the rhythm without someone reading the other parts.

## 2. Your scene partner

RehearseLines gives you a scene partner in your browser. You can organize a show, bring in a script, and hear the other characters read their lines while you practice yours.

## 3. Create a production

I'll start on the dashboard and create a production. This one is a play called The Clockwork Curtain. A production keeps this show's scenes, cast, and rehearsal settings together.

## 4. Import a script

Now I'll open Rehearse, go to Scenes, and choose Import Scenes. I'll paste a short original scene for this demo. You can also start with a file using Upload File.

## 5. Review the cast

I'll parse the text and check the character names before creating the scene. This gives me a chance to correct anything the app couldn't identify on its own.

## 6. Assign a voice

Over in Cast, there are the two characters, Mara and Eli. I can manage each character here. I'll open Eli's voice settings and give him a voice.

## 7. Set up a rehearsal

Now I'll open Run Lines, pick the scene, and choose Mara as my character. I'll leave it on Pause and wait, so I can take my turn at my own pace.

## 8. Run the scene

I'll start the scene. The app waits on Mara's line. When I'm ready, I'll move on and let it read Eli's reply.

## 9. Stored in this browser

One thing to know before you start your own show. Productions and imported scripts are saved locally in the browser you use. That keeps scripts private by default and helps avoid distributing copyrighted material.

## 10. Fresh browser · Microsoft Edge

Here's the same site in a fresh browser. The Clockwork Curtain isn't on this list. Switching browsers or devices doesn't bring your productions over automatically.

## 11. Transfer a production

You can move a production yourself. In Data Management, you can export a project and then import it in the other browser. We'll walk through that in a future tutorial.
